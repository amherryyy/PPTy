# Presentation design research: November 2026

Preference: explore a wide range of current presentation design references, including Philippine visual culture and playful anime, cartoon, pop, gradient, and illustrative directions when appropriate. Avoid recurring headers and footers by default. Choose the visual direction to suit the audience and subject; maintain readability, factual fidelity, and author control. This preference does not establish that one style is appropriate for every project.

## Research and direction

- Canva's 2026 trends discuss expressive surreal compositions, retro-tech and lo-fi aesthetics, and editorial or zine influences. These are global platform observations, not a ranked survey of Philippine presentation preferences. https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/design-trends-2026/
- Manila Bulletin reports Canva Philippines' perspective on expressive, personal work. The underlying survey cited there covers creators in the US and Brazil; do not present it as a Philippine-only study. https://mb.com.ph/2025/12/18/the-design-trends-set-to-supercharge-filipino-creativity-in-2026
- Philippine design publication adobo Magazine discusses local pixel and retro-tech influences, handmade lettering, doodles, and organic textures. Consider locally relevant references with context, rather than treating cultural motifs as interchangeable decoration. https://www.adobomagazine.com/featured/diskarte-design-2025s-creative-trends-for-filipino-artists-and-designers/
- Slidesgo identifies playful details, asymmetric composition, expressive typography, accessible hierarchy, and clear data storytelling as 2026 presentation directions. https://slidesgo.com/presentation-trends
- 24Slides' 2025 survey of presentation design directions includes lively gradients, retro palettes, illustration, and handcrafted textures. https://24slides.com/presentbetter/best-presentation-design-trends
- Canva's public education and technology galleries provide examples of playful, illustrated, retro, and bold presentation directions. https://www.canva.com/presentations/templates/education/ and https://www.canva.com/presentations/templates/technology/

## Candidate directions

1. Pop and anime-inspired: strong color blocking, original character-like illustrations, expressive framing, and comic panel sequencing; use readable text areas and avoid copying recognizable characters or proprietary layouts.
2. Playful classroom: illustrative motifs, friendly shapes, bright but controlled palettes, and large labeled diagrams.
3. Gradient digital: layered color transitions and high contrast content panels, with gradient placement selected to support hierarchy.
4. Filipino retro and craft: locally informed typography or pixel patterns, handmade textures, and original cutout illustrations when relevant to the subject and audience.
5. Editorial technical: varied grids, purposeful data diagrams, and strong type with occasional expressive accents.

For each project, review references, select a visual direction suited to the brief, create original layouts, check readability at presentation size, and review the entire deck. Source evidence and numerical claims remain traceable in the content model. Do not interpret generic web search results as a verified ranking or representative survey of Filipino presentation preferences.
