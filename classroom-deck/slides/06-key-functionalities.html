06

The Tools Students Use Most

Each feature supports a practical step in the study routine.

Subjects & Topics

Build a personal list of classes and the topics that need attention.

Helps with: keeping study tasks in one place.

Duno Focus

Sort topics using due dates, importance, difficulty, and progress.

Helps with: deciding what to work on next.

Duno Plan

Turn available time into adjustable study blocks.

Helps with: making a manageable plan.

Duno Guide

Browse brief study suggestions and relevant course outcomes when available.

Helps with: finding a useful way to practice.

Duno Timer

Track focus time, review the session, then save it when ready.

Helps with: building a record of study sessions.

Aralytics

See descriptive summaries of saved study activity over time.

Helps with: looking back at study habits.
