## Evidence and interpretation safeguards

- Convenience samples are described as participant findings, not population estimates.
- N=38 multiple-response percentages may exceed 100% in combination.
- PHPUnit's 123-test suite includes the 95 CodeIgniter feature tests; do not sum them as independent tests.
- 12 manual cases passed in the latest verification; 10 were tested earlier but not repeated.
- Student and IT professional ratings describe perceptions, not independent proof of security, reliability, performance, or learning outcomes.
- Local test results are not represented as complete hosted-environment verification.
