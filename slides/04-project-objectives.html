Project Objectives

The approved objectives, carried forward from Talas to Duno

General Objective

To develop a web-based academic companion that helps students organize, prioritize, plan, and monitor their study activities through personalized recommendations and academic analytics.

01

Specific Objective 1

To enable students to manage subjects, topics, deadlines, and academic progress while generating personalized study priorities and adjustable daily study plans.

02

Specific Objective 2

To provide suitable study method recommendations informed by relevant learning outcomes and CHED program standards, alongside a built-in timer for recording study sessions and optional academic wellness check-ins.

03

Specific Objective 3

To develop an interactive dashboard for monitoring study patterns, time distribution, and completed activities, supported by searchable records, printable reports, secure user accounts, and administrative functions.
